The Road to Cooperstown

This summer, the Gladiators (12U Black) are headed to the Cooperstown All-Star Village — one of the most prestigious stages in youth baseball, played in the birthplace of the game. A week of games, lodging, and travel for a full 12U roster adds up fast, and every sponsor and donation gets this team closer to the field. Partner with us and your name rides with this team all the way to Cooperstown.
